Background & Problem

Many people struggle to find jobs that match their qualifications. Grade 12 learners often don't know which universities or courses they qualify for, and job seekers spend hours searching scattered websites for vacancies.

High Youth Unemployment

Thousands of qualified young people can't find their first opportunity.

No Career Guidance

Few learners get personalised advice on what they qualify for.

Scattered Opportunities

Jobs, learnerships and courses live on dozens of separate sites.

Our Solution

CareerConnect SA is a web platform that helps users find education and employment opportunities based on their actual qualifications. Upload your Grade 12 results, certificates, diplomas or degree — the system recommends universities, courses, careers, internships, learnerships and jobs that match.
